Basic pickleball rules

  1. Serve underhand

    You only score when you are serving, and you only get one serve.

  2. Serve cross court

    Serve cross court from the right-hand court when your score is even, and from the left when it is odd.

    Score even: serve from the right

  3. Let it bounce

    At the start of play, the serve and the return of serve must each bounce once.

  4. No kitchen volleys

    You cannot volley the ball while standing in the kitchen, the yellow zone next to the net.

  5. Play to 11

    First to 11 points wins, but you have to win by 2.

House rules

A few basics so everyone gets a great game.

Respect and courtesy

Show respect and courtesy to others at all times. Please look after the facility and keep it clean and tidy while you play.

Equipment care

You are responsible for the paddles and balls provided. Please handle all equipment with care to prevent damage or loss.

Booking and court use

Courts are booked every hour, on the hour, and each booking gives you one hour of play. Bookings must be made in advance and are subject to availability.

Timeliness

Please arrive on time for your booking, and make way promptly for the next players when your hour is up.

Following the rules

Players must follow all posted rules and regulations and the instructions of the court staff.
